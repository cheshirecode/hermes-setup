Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 16, 2026 • Application: Hermes Agent (GCP Project: principal-lane-485806-i9)

1. Overview

Hermes Agent ("the Application", "we", "us", or "our") is an open-source autonomous assistant developed by cheshirecode. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have complete control over your data. This Privacy Policy describes how your information is handled when using Hermes Agent and its Google Workspace integrations (Google Drive and Google Sheets).

2. Information Collection and Access

Hermes Agent operates as a client-side application. When you connect Hermes Agent to your Google Account via OAuth 2.0, the application requests access strictly to the following scopes:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.readonly: To search and read documents, PDFs, and files in your Google Drive solely upon your explicit instruction.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/spreadsheets: To read, format, and modify Google Sheets spreadsheets upon your explicit command.

3. How We Use Information

Information accessed from your Google Drive is used exclusively to:

  • Perform file search and content extraction requested by you in prompt turns.
  • Read spreadsheet rows and columns to answer your specific analytical queries.
  • Enable local context indexing within your private Hermes Agent session.

Google API Services User Data Policy Compliance

Hermes Agent's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Data Storage and Security

All OAuth tokens and session data are stored locally on your device (in ~/.config/gdrive-mcp/ or your local container volume) with restricted file permissions (0600).

  • We do not operate external centralized databases storing your Google Drive content.
  • We do not transfer your personal files to external third-party servers.
  • We never use your Google Drive data to train machine learning models.

5. Information Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your Google user data with any third parties, advertisers, or commercial entities under any circumstances.

6. User Control and Data Revocation

You maintain total ownership and control of your data. You may revoke Hermes Agent's access to your Google Account at any time by visiting Google Account Permissions and removing access for the application. You can also delete local credential files on your machine by removing the ~/.config/gdrive-mcp/ directory.

7. Contact Information

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or the security practices of Hermes Agent, please open an issue on the official GitHub repository at https://github.com/cheshirecode/hermes-setup/issues.
